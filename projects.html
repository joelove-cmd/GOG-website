Our Projects

Feeding the hungry

"Sharing God’s love through a warm meal."

Feeding the Hungry is one of our core outreach programs aimed at addressing food insecurity among vulnerable children, youth, and families in our community. We believe that no child should go to bed hungry and that access to nutritious food is a basic right and a foundation for holistic development.

Spirituality

"Raising children and youth rooted in Christ." Proverbs 22:6

Our Spirituality Project is at the heart of everything we do. It aims to nurture the spiritual growth of children and youth by helping them build a strong relationship with Jesus Christ. We believe that true transformation begins from within, and a Christ-centered life empowers young people to live with purpose, integrity, and hope.

Care with Love

"Meeting practical needs with a heart full of compassion."

The Care with Love Project focuses on restoring dignity and hope to vulnerable children and youth by providing essential items such as shoes, clothes, hygiene kits, school supplies, and more. Every gift is a tangible expression of Christ’s love — reminding each child that they are seen, valued, and cared for.

Moments That Matter

"Because every child deserves a memory worth smiling about."

The Moments That Matter project is all about creating joyful, unforgettable experiences for children and youth. We believe that beyond meeting basic needs, children deserve to laugh, play, and feel celebrated. Through this project, we organize special moments that build confidence, connection, and lasting happiness.
