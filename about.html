About Us

Genesis of Galaxy is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing assistance to underprivileged and orphaned children and youth in Uganda. Our mission is to empower these children and youth by providing them with access to education, healthcare, and a nurturing environment. We strive to make a positive impact on their lives and give them hope for a better future.

Our organization was founded with the vision of making a lasting difference in the lives of these children. We believe in the power of compassion and the ability to transform lives. By supporting Genesis of Galaxy, you are contributing to a brighter tomorrow for these children and youth.

Our Mission, Vision, and Values

Mission

To be a Christ centered, home based child & youth focused organization.

Vision

Holistic development of children and youth

Values

Faith and Spirituality, Child-Centeredness, Family and Community, Compassion, Holistic Growth, and Empowerment.
